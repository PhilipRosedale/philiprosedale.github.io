Inequality

A free market simulation

Two hundred identical people trade at random: one pays the other a small random amount, at most 4% of what the poorer of the two has. Nobody is smarter or luckier, yet wealth drifts to the top and the middle class shrinks. The curve sorts everyone from poorest to richest and shows how much of the total wealth they hold.
