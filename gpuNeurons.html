Homeostasis

Fatigue raises a cell's threshold and drains its charge each time it fires, and fades over about 15 seconds. Inhibitory learning strengthens the inhibition onto any cell firing faster than 0.05/s and weakens it onto quieter ones. Compensate raises a cell's threshold whenever one of its inputs strengthens.
