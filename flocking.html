Flocking

Birds in a box. Each one is two triangles that flap along its direction of flight.
Separation, alignment, and cohesion use only the nearest neighbors inside a fixed search radius. The box is split into cells of that same size, so a bird looks through just the cells the radius can reach — at most a 3×3×3 block — instead of every other bird.
